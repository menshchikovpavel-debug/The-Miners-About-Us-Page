Our story

It startedin Prague.The idea wentfurther.

The Miners started in Prague in 2019 with a simple idea: create a place where great coffee feels easy, people feel comfortable being themselves, and there is always space for something new.

What started with one coffee shop has grown into a roastery, bakery, academy and dozens of locations across Europe — while keeping the same spirit at the center of the brand.

Founders: Egor Kolpakov & Oldřich Valta
